How to Play

1. Camera Mode

Allow camera access when prompted. Point your hand at the webcam and make a Rock ✊, Paper ✋, or Scissors ✌️ gesture. Click "Lock Image" or use "3-2-1 Shoot!" to lock your move.

2. Upload Mode (Camera Fallback)

If you don't allow camera access, or if you prefer uploading, switch to Upload Image Mode. Browse or drag-and-drop a photo of your hand gesture, then click "Play With This Image".

3. Opponent

The CPU will choose randomly once your move is locked. The winner is calculated immediately and the scoreboard updates automatically!
